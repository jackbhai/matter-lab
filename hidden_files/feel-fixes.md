## Fix #12 — Visual realism bar (user-set target)

Reference used as STYLE TARGET ONLY (clean-room; no assets/layout copied).

1. **Hero objects never hidden during the boil job — DONE.**
   - Root cause of the worst offender: main's `GlassShell.jsx` rendered the SETTINGS rail with class `toolwrap` while main's CSS ships it as `.mz-shell` + `[data-open="1"]` — no positioning matched, so a raw SETTINGS sheet sat in normal flow over/under the world at all times (visible in my own early after-shots, 256px of flow height). Wiring repair in `src/ui/GlassShell.jsx`: markup now uses the classes the shipped CSS expects (`mz-shell`, `data-open`, `mz-shell-hdr`, `mz-shell-title`); content unchanged; when `open` is undefined (App.jsx mounts conditionally) mounted means open. The rail is now a fixed off-canvas slide-in (verified: bounding rect x=412, fully off-screen when closed).
   - Mutual exclusivity: selecting gear hides the chip-strip card (`!selObj` in its render condition); opening a dock tab clears the selection (`setSelObj(null)` in openDockTab); the selection bar already required sheet/picker/side-panel closed.
   - Boil flow with no sheet: lighting a burner — by world tap (fix 5 toggle) or from its selection bar (`applyLevelFromBar`, off→on transition) — dismisses every contextual surface (selection cleared, dock tab cleared). Verified end-to-end in the hero session: after the LIGHT tap, selbar/sheet/strip all absent, burner at level 2, water boiling.
   - The SETTINGS sheet is not in the boil path (it never was functionally; it only *looked* omnipresent because of the wiring bug above).
2. **Burner = metal lab equipment, flame unmissable — DONE (with one honest limit).**
   - `burnerSprite` redrawn on the PR composition (pedestal + barrel + ring): cylindrical brushed-metal gradient across the barrel (dark edges, bright off-centre band), lit top face + dark front face on the base plate, plate bolts, gas inlet stub, radial-gradient control knob with notch, collar, bright top ring with dark jet hole. (sandlab-sprites.js)
   - Render-only overdraw in sandlab.jsx: the body is drawn 36% wider / 12% taller than its 16×15-cell stamp, base planted on the stamp's bottom edge. Engine geometry, stamps and hit areas unchanged — documented here per instructions.
   - Flame: procedural layered cone (outer orange → amber → pale core → blue Bunsen core at the jet), rooted on the ring; supersedes the sprite-frame flame. See R3 for vessel sizing.
   - Limit: the engine stamp is 16×15 cells, so at the fallback portrait tier the body is ~25px wide on screen; the overdraw + shading are what carry it. It reads as metal equipment next to a vessel (hero shot), not as a large appliance.
3. **Beaker glass at world scale — DONE.** On the PR beaker sprite: double-edge wall (a second inset stroke at 90%/94% scale, so the wall reads thick, not a hairline), a hard specular core inside the existing soft streak, on top of fix 10's rim/keyline/graduations (numbers legible at M/L — visible in hero shot: 50–250).
4. **Hero proof — `hero-boil-nopanels.png` (412×915).** Real flow: gear tiles → resize via the app's own Resize (beaker 2.6×; burner resize is refused by the engine while a vessel sits on it at ≥2.0, so the burner stays 1× + overdraw) → fill → Done → centre-tap to light (auto-dismiss fired) → boil. Frame shows: giant beaker with thick glass + legible graduations, translucent blue liquid with bright surface line, big layered flame spanning ~55% of the beaker's width licking its base, steam column erupting, ZERO panels/cards/sheets over the world, one-line reaction ticker at top (permitted by R5). Honest gaps vs the reference: the pair sits on the bench at the frame's bottom (the world is a tall portrait grid and gear lands on the bench; the app's wheel-zoom pans the anchor to the top of the frame, so a centre-frame zoom composition was not reachable in the harness), and the burner body is proportionally smaller than the reference's platform.

- **R1 HERO-FIRST — DONE.** Boil job passes with zero panels over the beaker: hero-boil-nopanels.png (see fix #12.1 for the mechanisms: GlassShell wiring repair, mutual exclusivity, light-to-dismiss).

- **R3 FLAME — DONE.** Layered cone (blue core at the jet, pale inner, amber mid, orange outer), rooted on the burner ring; **sized to the vessel**: when a vessel is seated on the burner, cone width = max(burner width, 55% of vessel width, capped at 80%) and height ×1.18 — in the hero shot the flame spans ~55% of the beaker's width and licks its base. - **R4 LIQUID — DONE (render layer).** Meniscus: the existing surface polyline now gets a soft glow pass (rgba(150,220,255,0.35), ~2.5px) under a brighter hard line (alpha 0.95), and the sub-surface refraction band alpha 0.16 → 0.26 — the liquid surface reads instantly against black (hero shot). Bubbles inside the liquid are the engine's real sim cells and already render; the cell raster itself is engine-owned (out of scope), so "translucency" is carried by the surface treatment + glass streaks rather than re-rendering liquid cells.
- **R5 REACTION INFO = ONE LINE AT TOP — DONE.** `data-ticker` line inside the stage top: latest reaction in equation form via the app's own `rxEntryLine` (uses the card's equation text when present, else "A + B → products"), accent-edged, pointer-events none, full opacity ~6s then dims to 0.45. Count: in-world reaction surfaces BEFORE = stacked feed cards/badge flood (99+); AFTER = **1 line**; the full Messages feed stays behind its Menu button, unchanged. Evidence: hero-boil-nopanels.png (ticker visible at top), messages-grouped.png (feed intact on demand).
- **R6 ONE ACCENT — DONE.** Unified to the app's own primary token **--ml-c-accent (green: #00FF9C dark theme / #34e0b0 light)**. Audit: kit.css already routes .btn / .chip.on / .cat.on through var(--green) + var(--grad), so the selection bar's primary buttons, brush-pill steppers and dock chips were already on-accent; the dock's active tab is a neutral surface (not a competing accent).
