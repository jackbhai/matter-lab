Matter Lab — Privacy

Plain language, no legal fog. Last updated with v3.

What data exists

Matter Lab saves your work only on your own device, in your browser's local storage: your saved scenes and recipes, favourite and recently used substances, custom substances you create, your theme and settings, and whether you have seen the intro demo. That is the whole list.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no cookies set by Matter Lab. Nothing you make in the lab is uploaded anywhere — the app even works fully offline after the first load.

Deleting your data

Clear the site's data in your browser settings (or uninstall the app) and every save is gone permanently. There is no server copy to ask us to delete, because there is no server copy.
